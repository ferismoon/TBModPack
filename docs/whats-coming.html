A livelier Nether

We’re looking at more variety in the creatures encountered while exploring, while keeping Incendium’s existing world generation.

AdventureZ is currently included for server testing. We’re reviewing how its encounters fit the pack; further mob additions are still being considered.

Fine-tuning exploration rewards

The new treasure system is part of version 1.3. We’ll use player feedback to refine variety, rarity and any remaining gaps in structure coverage.

Tell us what you’d like help learning or what would make the pack more enjoyable in Discord.
