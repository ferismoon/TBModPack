Version 1.3.3 — Farm animals, loot and recipe fixes

  • Fixed the broken adult cold pig model when Squish and VanillaBackport are installed together. Squish baby pigs are kept.
  • Harvesting mature flax with a Farmer’s Delight knife now gives straw alongside the usual flax drops.
  • Restored Supplementaries antique ink as an extra fishing drop: 20% on junk catches and 2% on treasure catches.
  • Added a 15% chance for eligible burning mobs killed by fire to drop ash. Supplementaries’ ash blacklist is respected.
  • Fixed the normal furnace recipe without changing the separate blackstone furnace.
  • Stopped unavailable rose gold tool recipes from loading when their required items are absent.
  • Corrected Create filling costs to half a bucket for rich soil and a quarter bucket for hot cocoa.
  • Corrected Necronomicon’s bundled mixin mapping reference. The client tooltip and F3 visual check is still pending.

Existing Eldritch End registry warnings are being left alone to preserve saved world data. Some bundled Supplementaries loot warnings can still appear even though the missing drops are supplied by our compatibility fix.

Version 1.3.2 � End fixes and compatibility cleanup

  • Dimensional Tears in the End now remove non-player entities without drops instead of gathering them at the central island. Player travel is unchanged.
  • Disabled parallel world generation to avoid the BetterEnd lake-generation crash.
  • Iron and diamond chisels now count as tools when crafting the Tokimi's Toolshed manual.
  • Cleaned up optional BloomingNature, NetherVinery and Epic Samurai references. Structures requiring missing blocks remain inactive.

Animal variants and their settings are unchanged.

20 September 2026 — End exploration fixes

  • Non-player entities entering Dimensional Tears in the End now disappear without drops instead of being teleported to the central island. Players keep their usual teleport behaviour. Existing entity piles need separate cleanup.
  • Disabled C2ME parallel world generation following a BetterEnd lake-generation crash. New terrain may generate more slowly.

20 September 2026 — A tougher, fairer dragon fight

The Ender Dragon fight has been tuned with player feedback and is now ready for everyone to take on, on the server or in single-player.

  • Less healing downtime: End crystals now return at twice the baseline interval. The dragon has 10% more health and deals 25% more damage, with difficulty kept at 2.
  • More reinforcements: Raised the mob multiplier from 1.25 to 2.5 and restored natural spawning on the main island. Reinforcements mix ordinary Endermen with End Endermen, Shadow Walkers, Enderwarthogs and occasional End Islands Endermen.
  • Crystal defenders: Phantoms appear when crystals are destroyed at range, and blazes defend returning crystals. Void Shades are no longer summoned during the fight.
  • Lightning disabled: Removed the fight's lightning attacks, which were killing reinforcements. Explosion attacks remain.
  • Easier melee targeting: Enlarged the dragon's head hitbox from one block to two blocks wide, tall and deep. Other body-part hitboxes are unchanged.
  • End mob balance: Enderwarthogs and Void Shades have 50% more base health and damage. Shades also have a 16-block firing range and faster flight. These changes apply outside the fight too; End Endermen retain their original health and damage after player testing.
  • Tranquil Lantern restriction: Players can no longer place any tier of Tranquil Lantern in the End. This does not disable lanterns already placed there or block automated placement.

Update the pack and restart Minecraft before playing. Servers must update and restart too. Existing mobs may retain saved stats; newly spawned mobs use the updated balance.

Version 1.3.1 — Elevators and stable barrel displays

  • OpenBlocks Elevator: Added elevator blocks for convenient travel between floors, using the Java 17-compatible 0.0.3 release. Visit the mod project page for details.
  • Sophisticated Storage fix: Added SophisticatedStorage_Fixes to stop barrel item displays flickering or showing neighbouring barrels’ items across chunk boundaries. Stored items are unaffected.

Version 1.3 — Treasure worth finding

Exploration rewards now draw from more of the pack, with useful supplies, collectible finds and occasional rare equipment across vanilla and modded structures.

  • Themed treasure: Expanded food, seeds, ingredients, metals, Create components and Hexalia brewing supplies, with rewards chosen to suit the location.
  • Lucky finds: Map chests can contain Nature’s or Explorer’s Compasses. Advanced materials and individual obsidian or netherite equipment pieces are rare rewards in suitable higher-tier chests.
  • Collectible plushies: Server player plushies join the shared chest reward pool. The new plushie roll can add at most one plushie per eligible chest, and broken Perfect Plushies loot references have been replaced.
  • Tidier chests: Our Loot Tidy mod combines matching stacks before filling supported Lootr containers and places items in slot order, reducing wasted space and overflow.
  • Modded structure coverage: Expanded reward support and container handling, including chests, trapped chests, barrels and shulker boxes provided through Lootr.
  • Loot system cleanup: Replaced Loot Integrations and its add-ons with our own loot setup, and moved earlier loot adjustments into that system. Mods’ existing loot additions are retained where appropriate; crash-prone Beachparty rings remain excluded.
  • Quest retirement: Removed FTB Quests and its quest currency mod, Numismatic Overhaul. Server player plushies can now be found through exploration.
  • Starter items: Removed the duplicate starter-kit source that was giving new players extra starting items.
  • AdventureZ — testing: Included for server testing to explore more creature and combat variety. Its fit and balance are still under review.

Loot changes apply when loot is newly generated; already generated inventories are not rewritten. Please report missing rewards, untidy containers or balance concerns in Discord.

Version 1.2.2 — Recovery and farming

  • Custom Disenchanter: A dedicated workstation for recovering selected enchantments onto books.
  • Uncraft Everything: An Uncrafting Table for recovering ingredients from supported crafted items.
  • Scarecrows’ Territory: Keep nearby spawners activated without standing beside them. The area must remain loaded; scarecrows do not load chunks themselves.

Project links for all three additions are available on the Mod Wikis page.

Version 1.2.1 — Dark UI fixes

  • Extended the dark theme to more modded screens, including storage, backpacks, Create machinery, and cooking stations.
  • Updated Sophisticated and Tom’s Storage interfaces to match their current layouts.
  • Fixed light slot backgrounds in Iron Chests and the white Accessories sidebar, tabs, and controls.

Version 1.2 — Server launch

True Bastards: Forced Relocation is live. A new home for the server, with more to discover, build, and collect.

  • More to explore: BetterEnd, Bosses of Mass Destruction, and an enhanced Ender Dragon fight expand the adventure.
  • A livelier world: More creeper, enderman, and other mob variants, plus pillager caravans and expanded wildlife spawning.
  • More building options: New Create machinery and storage additions, alongside Macaw’s Windows.
  • More rewarding loot: Village chests now include profession-themed modded items, with refreshed ocean loot and repeat-looting through Lootr chest resets.
  • Server player plushies: Collect familiar faces through the dedicated plushie quests.
  • Everyday improvements: REI replaces JEI for recipe browsing, and inventory sorting has been replaced with Sort It Out.
  • A refreshed look: Updated menus, resource packs, and support for custom mob models and animations.
  • Easier updates: The Prism Launcher instance checks for pack updates automatically at launch.

Some bugs remain and fixes are ongoing. Please report anything you find in Discord.
