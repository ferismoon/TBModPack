Build a home. Find your fortune.

Forced Relocation is a relaxed, long-term survival pack for friends who want more to explore, build, grow, automate, and occasionally fight.

Build a settlement, raise farms, build Create machines, discover strange biomes, brave forgotten ruins, and prepare for bosses beyond the Overworld.

Choose your path

  • World generation: BetterEnd, Regions Unexplored, Oh The Biomes We’ve Gone, Tectonic, Incendium, YUNG’s structures, and new villages and ruins.
  • Automation: Create, Steam ’n’ Rails, Create: Addition, Create: Deco, Create: Jetpack, Copycats+, and storage networks.
  • Cooking & farming: Farmer’s Delight, the Let’s Do food and farming mods, Nature’s Delight, Nether’s Delight, Ocean’s Delight, and End’s Delight.
  • Magic & potions: Hexalia, enchanting improvements, potion information, and new magical ingredients.
  • Gear: Advanced Netherite, Mythic Upgrades, BetterEnd equipment, backpacks, gliders, tools, and expanded weapons.
  • Illagers & village defence: Illager Invasion, Pillager Caravans, IllagerBlabber, and Guard Villagers bring more foes, encounters, chatter, and defenders to the world.
  • Mob variants: Enderman Overhaul, Creeper Overhaul, and Variants & Ventures add more variety to familiar mobs. Explore the mob guides and project pages.
  • Mobs & bosses: Bosses of Mass Destruction, Deeper and Darker, Savage Ender Dragon, Illager Invasion, Friends & Foes, and new wildlife.
  • Utilities: REI, Jade, Waystones, map tools, graves, backpacks, quests, and quality-of-life improvements.
  • Claims & teams: FTB Teams, Open Parties and Claims, FTB Essentials, and shared server tools.

Ready to join?
